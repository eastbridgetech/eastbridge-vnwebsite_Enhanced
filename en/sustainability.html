Five design principles

Sustainability is incorporated into architecture, engineering and operations without compromising security, accessibility or business outcomes.

01

Value before compute

Use technology only where the expected outcome justifies data, infrastructure and operational cost.

02

Efficient by design

Reduce unnecessary processing, storage, transfer, duplication and always-on resources.

03

Responsible AI

Select proportionate models, protect data, evaluate quality and retain meaningful human oversight.

04

Lifecycle thinking

Design for maintainability, reuse, observability, retirement and responsible change.

05

Measure and improve

Establish baselines, monitor practical indicators and prioritize the highest-value improvements.

Green software engineering

Performance, cost and environmental efficiency often reinforce each other. EastBridge uses engineering practices that improve all three.

  • Performance budgets for pages, APIs, integrations and batch jobs
  • Efficient algorithms, data access and network transfer
  • Right-sized cloud services, autoscaling and idle-resource control
  • Observability for utilization, latency, failures and capacity
  • Reusable components and longer useful software life
  • Release, archive and retirement processes that limit digital clutter

Sustainable AI by design

AI should be useful, proportionate, governable and efficient across its full lifecycle.

Choose the smallest adequate approach

Compare rules, search, analytics, smaller models and larger models against the actual task before scaling compute.

Improve data and prompt efficiency

Minimize unnecessary context, duplicate inference and data movement while protecting quality and privacy.

Evaluate before production

Test accuracy, safety, latency, cost, human workflow and operational impact with representative scenarios.

Human oversight and accountability

Define review points, escalation, decision ownership and audit evidence for material use cases.

Monitor the model lifecycle

Track versions, usage, drift, failures, resource demand and retirement decisions.

Protect people and data

Apply data minimization, access control, secure integration and clear user communication.
