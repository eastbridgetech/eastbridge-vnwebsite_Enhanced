The EastBridge R&D lifecycle

Every initiative advances through evidence-based gates.

01

Discover

Define the user, problem, baseline and desired business outcome.

02

Research

Assess technology options, data, risk, sustainability and market context.

03

Prototype

Test architecture, workflow and experience with controlled effort.

04

Pilot

Measure usability, value, safety, performance and adoption with real users.

05

Productize

Complete quality, security, licensing, documentation and support readiness.

06

Expand through repeatable delivery while feeding evidence back into the roadmap.
